Daybook privacy policy

Last updated 21 September 2026.

Daybook is a personal application used only by its author, István Csapó, on accounts he owns.

What it accesses

  • Google Calendar: events, to draw the day's schedule.
  • Gmail: messages, to find deliveries, invoices and mail that needs a reply, and to apply labels to them.

Where the data goes

Everything the application reads stays on the author's own computer. Nothing is sent to third parties, sold, shared or used for advertising. Google user data is used only to display the author's own information to himself.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept in local files under the author's control and can be deleted at any time by deleting those files. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited use

Daybook's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
